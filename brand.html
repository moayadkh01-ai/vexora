Introduction

The VEXORA Identity

VEXORA (فيكسورا) is an international premium gaming platform. The identity is built on one idea: a hexagon arena carved with the V — a shield for competition, a vortex of energy. Deep space navy sets the premium stage; the violet→cyan energy gradient powers every accent; gold is reserved exclusively for VEXORA Coins and VIP.

5 Rules

✓ Do

Keep the hexagon intact, give the logo breathing room equal to the V's width, use the energy gradient for action and CTAs, pair VEXORA with فيكسورا on bilingual touchpoints.

✕ Don't

Never rotate or stretch the mark, never recolor the gradient, never use gold for non-monetary UI, never place the primary logo on busy imagery without a dark scrim, never abbreviate to "VX" in formal copy.
